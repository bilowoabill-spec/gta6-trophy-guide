Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off your trophy save. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5

    Play the missions and take side content as it appears. In Chapter 1, do not skip Lucia’s balcony meditation the morning after Down to Earth — it is the one trophy that may be missable. Turn on progress and collectible notifications in Settings, and scan vehicles and WAiNK spots as you travel.

    Earns: all 12 story trophies + the final-mission gold.

  2. Stage 2 — Character quests & strangers

    Finish the quest lines of Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, then 8 qualifying stranger strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count; Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie do not). None of these are needed for 100%, and all stay open after the story.

    Earns: 6 quest trophies + Strangers & Freaks.

  3. Stage 3 — 100% completion (A Long Road)

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through it: all robberies (8 bank vaults, 39 gang operations), underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold, all drone races, Bret’s 15 Target Decks — plus 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans. Around 100 hours, the longest stretch.

    Earns: the gold A Long Road, and most collectible and activity trophies along the way.

  4. Stage 4 — Clean-up & platinum

    Mop up the crime, money and one-off trophies (vaults, armored trucks, VCIA jet, tank, jewelry, garages, Scooter Bros), then check this list for anything left. The platinum pops with the last one.

    Earns: Vice Got a Price!

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (the morning after Down to Earth) disappears after that chapter, and it is not yet confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it, and keep a manual save.

Roadmap sources: GTABase trophy guide & roadmap — gtabase.com; PowerPyx via KeenGamer (~200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one possibly missable trophy); OutputLag trophy list (no mission replay, manual saves).
