Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — about 200 hours, most of it the 100% checklist — if you mind the one thing that can be missed. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10: long, not hard.

  1. Step 1 — Before you start. Leave cheats off: they switch off trophies and saving on that save. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots plus one autosave; there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus). In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony at Jason’s house and accept her meditation — see Positive Mental Attitude.
  2. Step 2 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5. The 12 chapter and named-mission trophies pop as you go (chapter trophies can pop at the start of the next chapter). Character switching opens in Chapter 2: from then on, call your partner in for the partner trophies and start scanning vehicles with WAiNK as you drive.
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & side finales. Finish 8 stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks (Andy’s, Billy’s and Tyrone’s strands do not count — they have their own trophies). Do the time-sensitive Chapter 3 race Race Against Yago the moment it appears. The six side-story finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) all stay open after the credits.
  4. Step 4 — Robberies & money. Chapter 3: the Snap Gun from Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs opens jewelry robberies (Sneakthief). Chapter 4: That’s Where The Money Is opens bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal). Rob one illegal operation per gang (Pest Control), launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, buy the 8 garages and register 10 vehicles.
  5. Step 5 — Cleanup & 100%. After the credits the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion: 300 stunts, 70 of 80 Challenges, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 15 of 18 meditations, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans. Finish it for A Long Road — and the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops with it.

⚠ Missable: exactly one trophy can be missed — Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot). Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears when Chapter 1 ends, and it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs it. Do it, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.
